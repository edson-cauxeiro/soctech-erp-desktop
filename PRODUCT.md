# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, store managers, cashiers, and financial operators managing retail, wholesale, and service operations.

## Product Purpose

A desktop ERP suite designed for fast, reliable daily business operations — covering Point of Sale (POS), sales management, inventory/stock control, purchasing, tax/fiscal compliance, and financial accounting.

## Positioning

A responsive, robust local desktop ERP application built with Electron, React, TypeScript, and Prisma, providing high-speed offline-capable transaction processing, real-time stock updates, and structured fiscal reporting.

## Operating Context

- **Environment**: Desktop workstations and point-of-sale terminals in retail stores, warehouses, and offices.
- **Key Modules**:
  - **Início (Dashboard)**: Daily revenue, sales trends, transaction metrics, and fast shortcuts.
  - **Vendas & POS**: Quick item checkout, barcode scanning, payment processing, invoice generation.
  - **Estoque (Inventory)**: SKU tracking, stock adjustments, low-stock alerts, warehouse management.
  - **Compras (Purchases)**: Supplier orders, goods receipt, purchase bills.
  - **Finanças (Finance)**: Cash flow, accounts receivable/payable, banking, transaction logs.
  - **Fiscal**: Tax categories, invoice certification, compliance documents.
  - **Relatórios (Reports)**: Analytical reporting on sales, profit margins, inventory valuation.
  - **Configurações (Settings)**: Company details, user roles/permissions, device/printer settings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack**: Electron, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Radix UI primitives, Lucide icons, Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL.
- **Language & Localization**: Portuguese (primary UI language and business terminology: Vendas, Estoque, Fornecedores, Clientes, Facturação/Faturamento).
- **Performance**: Instant UI feedback for keyboard-driven cashier workflows and high-volume product catalogs.

## Product Principles

1. **High Efficiency & Scanability**: Prioritize information hierarchy and keyboard navigation for rapid data entry and POS workflows.
2. **Operational Reliability**: Clear visual states for transactions, stock balances, and fiscal operations to prevent costly business errors.
3. **Consistency & Native Desktop Polish**: Clean, modern interface using structured design tokens, cohesive typography, and purposeful micro-interactions.
